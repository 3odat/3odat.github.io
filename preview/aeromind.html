AeroMind: Poisoning the Shared-Memory Control Plane of an LLM-Driven Multi-UAV System

Ibrahim Odat, Anyi Liu, Yao Qiang (Oakland University), Yingjiu Li (University of Oregon)

When an LLM-driven UAV fleet coordinates through shared memory, that memory becomes the control plane. A compromised perception or telemetry feed on one UAV can write schema-valid false records through the fleet's trusted ingest path, and UAVs that never sensed the data act on them. We build AeroMind, an end-to-end testbed with a coordination simulator and a PX4, Gazebo, and ROS 2 software-in-the-loop stack, and evaluate the attack across six LLM backends and fleets of up to 32 UAVs.
